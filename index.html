About Cyberdog

A global intelligence network, built to protect the digital world.

Founded in 2023, Cyberdog was built to close the gap between the speed of modern cybercrime and the resources available to stop it — a coordinated network of analysts, investigators, researchers, and technologists operating under a single, unwavering mission.

Our purpose is simple and absolute: identify digital threats, investigate cybercriminal activity, and support lawful efforts to protect individuals, organizations, and communities from harm.

We operate as a disciplined intelligence and investigations network — never vigilantes, never hackers. Every member operates within the law, guided by process, evidence, and accountability.

Our Process

How We Work

A disciplined, repeatable process — from first signal to lawful action.

  1. 01

    Intelligence Collection

    We gather signals from across the surface, deep, and dark web — monitoring for indicators of cybercriminal activity, emerging threats, and targeted harm.

  2. 02

    Analysis

    Trained analysts assess, verify, and contextualize raw intelligence — separating signal from noise and prioritizing what matters.

  3. 03

    Investigation

    Verified leads are developed into structured investigations, built on evidence, documentation, and sound methodology.

  4. 04

    Collaboration

    Findings are shared with appropriate partners — law enforcement, platforms, and organizations — to support lawful, effective action.
